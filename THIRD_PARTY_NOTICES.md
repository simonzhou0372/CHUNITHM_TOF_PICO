## Pololu VL53L0X Arduino Library

https://github.com/pololu/vl53l0x-arduino

The VL53L0X initialization sequence in `src/vl53l0x.cpp`
is derived from the Pololu VL53L0X Arduino library.

The Pololu library is substantially based on ST's VL53L0X API.

The remaining RP2040-specific driver implementation,
multi-sensor management, XSHUT/address management,
data-ready handling and error handling were independently
implemented for Chunitof Pico.
